For You ❤️

The Only Star in My Universe

June eighth, two thousand twenty-three,
A train moved north, and so did fate.
Steel tracks hummed beneath our feet,
Unknowing hearts arrived too late
To pretend this was just chance.

We wore the same uniform,
Yet you stood apart —
Not louder, not brighter,
Just gentler.
You watched if I ate,
If I rested,
If loneliness ever brushed past me.
Without asking,
You chose to care.

Eleven p.m. found me awake,
On an upper berth of quiet thoughts,
Scrolling through nothing,
Feeling everything.
You looked up and asked,
“Would you like to join us?”

I said yes —
And somehow, the world narrowed
Until it was only you and me.

From eleven to four,
Time forgot itself.
Words spilled like they had been waiting
Across lifetimes.
No sleep.
No silence.
Just two souls learning each other
Under dim lights and passing stars.
That night still breathes inside my heart.

At camp, we danced.
Not for rhythm, not for show —
But because closeness felt natural,
And joy found its way to our hands.

June twenty-first arrived too soon.
Suitcases stood where courage should have been.
That goodbye hurt
Because love had already taken root.
We cried,
Not because it was ending,
But because we knew
It wasn’t meant to.

Distance tried its hand at us —
Late calls, glowing screens,
Voices wrapped around midnight.
We were inseparable,
Just measured in miles.

September first.
Music waiting in the dark.
Then — a kiss.
Gentle. Certain.
Like truth finally spoken aloud.

You are my man.
My safe place.
My once-in-a-lifetime.

And in a universe filled with stars,
You are the only one
My heart ever learned to orbit.
